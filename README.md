# AIONOS — Customer Resolution Agent

> **An intelligent, policy-aware customer resolution platform for airline support operations.**

AIONOS is a customer resolution assistant designed to help support teams analyze customer requests, retrieve relevant booking context, evaluate applicable policies, and recommend the appropriate resolution.

Instead of simply generating conversational responses, AIONOS separates **customer intent, contextual information, deterministic policy evaluation, resolution, and escalation**.

---

## Overview

Customer service requests often involve more than answering a simple question.

A single customer may ask for:

- A refund
- Rebooking
- Compensation
- Meal vouchers
- Lounge access
- Hotel accommodation
- Fare-difference waivers
- Upgrades

Determining the correct response requires understanding the customer's situation and applying the appropriate operational policies.

### AIONOS addresses this through a structured resolution workflow:

```text
Customer Request
       ↓
Intent Understanding
       ↓
Customer & Booking Context
       ↓
Policy Evaluation
       ↓
Eligibility Decision
       ↓
Resolution / Escalation
